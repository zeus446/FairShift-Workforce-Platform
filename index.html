          FairShift — 2-Minute Demo Script

          1. Switch Scenario: Toggle between Construction and College Facilities — compare real seeded roles, shifts, and safety reports.
          2. Worker Check-In: Pick a worker, record a check-in and check-out, then see actual recorded hours update pay and safety checks.
          3. FairPay Updates Live: After 8h, overtime (1.5×) kicks in. Deductions 12%. Net pay calculates automatically.
          4. FatigueGuard Alerts: See clear rule-based explanations for shifts over 12h, rest under 11h, or more than 60h in a week.
          5. SafeVoice Hazard Report: Worker submits hazard (anonymous option) → appears in their list as "Submitted".
          6. Employer Resolves: Switch to Employer view, acknowledge or investigate a report, then resolve it and confirm the worker sees the new status.
          7. Fairness Pulse: Dashboard shows unresolved hazards, overtime %, fatigue alerts, workforce table with risk flags.
          8. Language Switch: EN / HI / TA — all strings swap instantly, persists across reloads.
          9. Reset Demo: One click restores clean seeded state for next demo.

          All data stays in this browser. No backend or secure anonymity. Built for SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth.
